How to read this

  • The data is generated, not real sales. Promotions carry an extra lift beyond the discount itself, so a price-only fit overstates sensitivity. The controlled fit recovers the true values far more closely.
  • Demand is modeled with constant elasticity around each product's 26-week average price and units. That average includes promo weeks, so reference prices sit slightly below list.
  • Profit means gross profit: price minus unit cost, times units. Fixed costs, cannibalization between products and competitor reactions are not modeled.
  • Treat moves outside the observed price range as untested. Confidence is low where the interval for the elasticity comes close to zero.
